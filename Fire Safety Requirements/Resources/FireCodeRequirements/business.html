FIRE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS OF BUSINESS OCCUPANCY
REQUIREMENTS FIRE CODE REQUIREMENTS
1. Means of Egress
(Fire Exit)
a. Number
(Means of Egress)

Atleast 2 exit remote from each other

(SECTION 10.2.16.2 para D)
b. Capacity
(Means of Egress)
  1. 7.6mm stairway; 5mm level Components and Ramps (width per person)
    (SECTION 10.2.5.2 para C)
  2. The clear width of any corridor or passageway serving an occupant load of fifty (50) or more shall be not less than 1.12 meters.

    a. It is not the intent that this provision apply to non-corridor or nonpassageway areas of exit access, such as the spaces between rows of desks created by office layout or low-height partitions.

  3. Street floor exits shall be sufficient for the occupant load of the street floor plus the required capacity of stairs and ramps discharging through the street floor.
(SECTION 10.2.16.2 para C)
c. Occupant Load
(Means of Egress)

The Occupant load factor for the general use shall not be less than 1 person per 9.3 sq. m. of gross floor area and for concentrated use as in the case of Call Centers, IT Centers, BPO’s and other similar occupancies, shall not be less than 1 person per 4.6 sq. m. of gross floor area.

(SECTION 10.2.16.1 para C.1)
d. Size
(Means of Egress) 		Not more than 915 mm
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para F)
e. Stairwidth
(Means of Egress) 		New Stait Width
< 500 persons - 915mm
< 2000 persons - 1120mm
≥ 2000 persons - 1420mm
(SECTION 10.2.5.4 para B.2)

f. Arrangement/ Remoteness
(Means of Egress)
  1. Without supervised automatic sprinkler system - 1/2 of the maximum overall diagonal dimension
  2. With supervised automatic sprinkler system - 1/3 of the maximum overall diagonal dimension
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para H)
g. Travel Distance
(Means of Egress)
  1. Without supervised automatic sprinkler system  • not exceeding by 46m
  2. With supervised automatic sprinkler system  • not exceeding by 61m
h. Discharge
(Means of Egress)
  1. Terminate directly at a public way or at an exterior exit discharge.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.2 para M)
  2. Exits may also discharge on the areas of the floor exit discharge provided the following are met:
    1. Such exits discharge to a free and unobstructed way to the exterior of the building by not more than 6m;
    2. floor of discharge into which the exit discharge is provided with AFSS;
    3. entire area on the floor of discharge is separated from areas below by construction having a minimum fireresistance rating of two (2) hours.
(SECTION 10.2.16.2 para G)
2. Door
  1. Doors in means of egress shall swing in the direction of exit travel.
  2. Not less than 71 cm in clear width.
  3. No single door in a doorway shall exceed 122 cm in width.
(SECTION 10.2.5.3)

3. Lightings and Signs
  1. Signs designating exit or ways of travel
    (SECTION 10.2.5.12 para A)
  2. Exit Lighting
    (SECTION 10.2.5.12 para B)
  3. Emergency Lighting
    (SECTION 10.2.5.11 para C)
4. Protection of Vertical Openings

Vertical openings shall be enclosed or protected.

(SECTION 10.2.16.3 para A)
5. Fire Detection, Alarm and Communication System 1. Fire alarm system is required if any of the following exists:
 • 2 or more storeys in height above the level of exit discharge.  •50 or more occupants above or below the level of exit discharge. For existing building, 100 or more occupants above or below the level of exit discharge.  • 300 or more occupants. For existing building, 1000 or more total occupants.
(SECTION 10.2.16.3 para D)
6. Automatic Fire Suppression System (AFSS)

Building is 15m or more in heights.

(SECTION 10.2.16.3 para C)
7. Fire Extinguisher
  • Low hazard - One unit per 200 square meters, travel distance of 15m
  • Moderate hazard - One unit per 100 square meters, travel distance of 12m
  • High hazard - One unit per 75 square meters 10 meters of max travel distance to extinguisher.
(SECTION 10.2.6.9 para G)
8. Hazardous Storage High hazard contents areas. High hazard contents areas, as classified in Division 4, shall meet the following criteria:
  1. The area shall be separated from other parts of the building by fire barriers having a fire resistance rating of not less than 1 hour, with all openings therein protected by ¾-hour, fire protection rated, self-closing fire door assemblies.
  2. The area shall be protected by an automatic extinguishing system.
(SECTION 10.2.16.3 para E.3)
9. Emergency Evacuation Plan

Shall be provided in accordance with Section 10.2.5.13 of the RIRR

(SECTION 10.2.16.2 para I)
