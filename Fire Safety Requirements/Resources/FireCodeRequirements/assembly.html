FIRE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS OF PLACES OF ASSEMBLY
REQUIREMENTS FIRE CODE REQUIREMENTS
1. Means of Egress (Fire Exit)
a. Number (Means of Egress)
  1. At least 2 exit remote from each other
  2. Atleast 3 exits remote from each other for 500 persons but not more than 1000 persons
  3. Atleast 4 exits remote from each other for 1000 persons or more
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para G)
b. Capacity (Means of Egress)
  1. If risers are equal to or less than 178mm in height, A=1, In excess of 178 mm height, multiply the stair width in table 9 by factor A where A = 1 + [(RISER HEIGHT – 178)/125)]
    2. TABLE 9: CAPACITY FACTORS FOR THEATER TYPE SEATING
    No of Seats Clear width per seat served (in mm)
    Stairs Passageways, Ramps, and Doorways
    Unlimited 7.6 AB 5.6 C
  2. Stairs without a handrail located within a 760 mm horizontal distance shall be 25 percent wider than otherwise calculated, that is, multiply by factor B = 1.25. If handrails are provided within 760 mm, B=1
  3. Ramps steeper than 1 in 10 slope where used in ascent shall have their widths increased by 10 percent, that is, multiply by factor C =1.10. If the ramp is within or less than One (1) in ten (10) in slope C=1
(SECTION 10.2.8.2 A)
c. Seating, Aisles and Railings (Means of Egress)
  1. Seating

    a.) The aisle access way between rows of seating shall have a clear width of not less than 305 mm and this minimum shall be increased by 7.6 mm for every seat over a total of 14 but shall not exceed 560 mm;

    b.) Rows of seating served by aisles or doorways at both ends shall not exceed 100 seats per row;

    c. ) Rows of seating served by aisles or doorways at one end only shall have a path of travel not exceeding 9m in length, measured from the farthest seat to an aisle.

  2. Aisles

    a) Serving more than 60 seats
      •not less than 915mm when serving only 1 side
      •not less than 122cm when serving both sides

    b) Serving less than 60 seats
      •not less than 76 cm wide

    c) Aisles shall terminate in a cross aisle, foyer or exit. The width(cross aisle, foyer or exit) shall not be less than (width of the widest aisle + 50% (total required width of the remaining aisles)

    d) No dead end aisle shall be greater than 6.1 m

    e) ≥ 46m travel distance

  3. Railings

    a) Not less than 66cm high above the floor for fasciae of boxes, balconies and galleries.

    b) Not less than 76cm width of aisles or 91 cm high if at foot steps for railings at ends of aisles extending to the fasciae

    c) Not less than 66 cm high for cross aisle

    (SECTION 10.2.8.2 para H)
