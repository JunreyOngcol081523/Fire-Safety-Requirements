c. Seating, Aisles and Railings
(Means of Egress)
  1. Seating

    a.) The aisle access way between rows of seating shall have a clear width of not less than 305 mm and this minimum shall be increased by 7.6 mm for every seat over a total of 14 but shall not exceed 560 mm;

    b.) Rows of seating served by aisles or doorways at both ends shall not exceed 100 seats per row;

    c. ) Rows of seating served by aisles or doorways at one end only shall have a path of travel not exceeding 9m in length, measured from the farthest seat to an aisle.

  2. Aisles

    a) Serving more than 60 seats
      •not less than 915mm when serving only 1 side
      •not less than 122cm when serving both sides

    b) Serving less than 60 seats
      •not less than 76 cm wide

    c) Aisles shall terminate in a cross aisle, foyer or exit. The width(cross aisle, foyer or exit) shall not be less than (width of the widest aisle + 50% (total required width of the remaining aisles)

    d) No dead end aisle shall be greater than 6.1 m

    e) ≥ 46m travel distance

  3. Railings

    a) Not less than 66cm high above the floor for fasciae of boxes, balconies and galleries.

    b) Not less than 76cm width of aisles or 91 cm high if at foot steps for railings at ends of aisles extending to the fasciae

    c) Not less than 66 cm high for cross aisle

    (SECTION 10.2.8.2 para H)
d. Occupant Load
(Means of Egress)
  1. concentrated use without fixed seats

    •0.65 square meter per person.

  2. less concentrated use

    •1.4 square meter per person

  3. Standing room or waiting space

    •0.28 square meter per person

  4. having fixed seats

    • determined by the number of fixed seats installed

  5. not having fixed seats

    • shall have the occupant load of the room posted on a conspicious place near the main exit from the room. Approved signages shall be maintained in legible manner

  6. waiting space area

    • 0.25 square meter per person

  7. areas not in excess of 930 square meter

    • the occupant load shall not exceed one person in 0.46 square meter areas in excess of 930 square meter

    • the occupant load shall not exceed one person in 0.65 square meter

    8. (SECTION 10.2.8.1)

h. Discharge
(Means of Egress)

Terminate directly at a public way or at an exterior exit discharge. (SECTION 10.2.5.2 para M)

2. Door
  1. Any door in a means of egress shall be of the side-hinged or pivoted- swinging type and shall swing in the direction of egress travel where serving a room or area with an occupant load of fifty (50) or more persons
  2. Capable of swinging from any position to the full required width of the opening in which it is installed.
  3. Swing in the direction of egress travel when used in an exit enclosure
  4. Doors shall not open directly onto a stair without a landing.
  5. The landing shall have a width not less than the width of the door.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.3 para B)
  6. Not less than 71 cm in clear width
  7. No single door in a doorway shall exceed 122 cm in width
    (SECTION 10.2.5.3 para E)

3. Lightings and Signs
  1. Illumination of means of egress.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.11)
  2. All signs must be illuminated. “EXIT”, “NOT AN EXIT”,”TO BASEMENT”, “STOREROOM”,”LINEN CLOSET” with directional signs.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.12)
  3. Exit sign must be not less than 15cm height and 19 mm width; In existing buildings not less than 11.5 cm height.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.12 para C)
  4. All places of assembly shall be provided with emergency lighting.

    5. (SECTION 10.2.8.2 para I)
4. Protection of Vertical Openings
  1. Special provisions for escalatorsopenings. Any escalator shall have its floor opening enclosed or protected
  2. Sprinkler vent method

    Escalator openings may be protected by the “sprinkler-vent” , consisting of combination of an automatic or smoke detection, automatic exhaust system and an automatic water curtain.

  3. Spray Nozzle Methods

      1. Spray nozzles shall be in the open type and have a solid conical spray pattern with discharged angles between 45 and 90 degrees

      2. Spray nozzles shall discharge at minimum pressure of 172 KPA.

  4. Partial Enclosure Method

    Construction providing fire resistanceequivalent with openings therein protected by approved self closing fire doors.

  5. Firestopping

      1. Every exterior and interior wall and partition shall be firestopped at each level.

      2. Unoccupied attic space shall be subdivided by firestops into areas not to exceed 280 square meters.

      3. Any concealed space between the ceiling and the floor shall be firestopped.

    (SECTION 10.2.6.5)
  6. Any Vertical Opening Shall be enclosed or protected.
    (SECTION 10.2.8.8 para A)
  7. Potential Hazard must be separated by fire barriers/construction with at least 1 hour rating.
    (SECTION 10.2.8.8 para C)

5. Fire Detection, Alarm and Communication System
  1. Manual - All assembly occupancies.
  2. Automatic - Occupant load exceeding 300 persons.
    3. (SECTION 10.2.8.8 para D)
6. Automatic Fire Suppression System (AFSS)

Following assembly occupancy with occupant load of 150 persons or more

  1. Bars with live entertainment
  2. Dance Halls
  3. Discotheques
  4. Assembly occupancy with festival seating.
  5. With occupant load of more than 300 persons:

    • throughout the storey containing the assembly occupancy;

    • throughout all storeys below the storey containing the assembly occupancy;

    • in case of an assembly occupancy located below the level of exit discharge, throughout all storeys intervening between that storey and the level of exit discharge, including the level of exit discharge.

(SECTION 10.2.8.8 para E)
7. Smoke Management

Public assembly buildings, structures or facilities with more than (14) seats shall be provided with smoke management system in such a way that the atmosphere shall not induce more than the acceptable limit by volume of the contaminated atmosphere emanating from the fire area. (SECTION 10.2.8.8 para F)

8. Fire Extinguisher
  1. Low hazard - 1 unit per 200 square meters, travel distance 15m
  2. Moderate hazard - 1 unit per 100 square meters, travel distance 12m
  3. High hazard - 1 unit per 75 square meters 1 unit every 10 meters of travel distance
(SECTION 10.2.6.9 para G)
9. Standpipe

The design, installation and maintenance of standpipe systems shall be in accordance with NFPA 14, Standard for the Installation of Standpipe, Private Hydrant and Hose Systems.
(SECTION 10.2.6.8 para A)

10. Hazardous Areas
  1. All hazard greater than the normal occupancy shall be enclosed with construction of at least 4 hours fire resistance rating.
    (SECTION 10.2.6.10 para A)
  2. At least 2 hours fire resistance rating for all construction enclosure for hazardous storage With self closing or automatic fire door.
    (SECTION 10.2.6.10 para B)
11. Staged and Enclosed Platforms
  1. Staged area / enclosed platform larger than 46.5 m² shall be protected by AFSS and ventilated in or above it.
  2. The proscenium opening shall have a fire resistant curtain to prevent glow with a 5 minute period.
  3. Every stage shall be provided with a fire control station located on or adjoining the stage.
  4. Auxilliary stage shall be less than 15.25m within any auxilliary space from a door providing to an exit.
  5. Auxilliary stage must have at least 2 exits, travel distance of 23 m, and common path of travel of 6 meters.
  6. Each stage shall be equipped with 38.1 millimeters wet standpipe with at least 30m hose on each side of the stage.

    7. (SECTION 10.2.8.3)

12. Decorative Material
  1. No decorative material which will ignite when exposed to a flame test shall be allowed in places of assembly.
  2. Flame test must be conducted from a common match and held in horizontal position 12.7 millimeters underneath the piece for a minimum of 15 seconds.
  3. Treatment may be applied to decorative material to accomplish flame proofing and such treatment shall be as renewed as often as necessary.
    4. (SECTION 10.2.8.5)
13. Pyroxylin Coated Fabric

Pyroxylin coated fabrics shall be restricted as follows:

  1. Nitrocelluse coating shall not exceed 430 grams for every square meter of fabric. (SECTION 10.2.8.6)
  2. total surface area of fabric complying para “a” shall not exceed 1m² for every 4.5m³ of room volume.
  3. exceeding 47 grams per m², the total area shall not exceed 1m² for every 2.25m³ of volume.
    (SECTION 10.2.8.6)
14. Motion Picture Screens, Stage Curtains and Drapes

No motion picture screens or screen masking curtains, buntings or other draperies shall be used unless the material is flame-proofed and will pass the match-flame
(SECTION 10.2.8.7)

15.Emergency Evacuation Plan And Occupant Load Capacity

A. Emergency evacuation plan, in accordance with Section 10.2.5.13 of this RIRR, shall be submitted to the C/MFM having jurisdiction.

B. In all theaters, nightclubs, dance halls and similar occupancies, an audible announcement and other means of announcement shall be made prior to the performance describing the location of exits to be used in case of fire or other emergencies.

(SECTION 10.2.8.10)

16. Standby Firemen
  1. Deploy standby firemen if it is essential for public safety.
  2. The Fireman shall be in uniform and remain on duty during the activity.

(SECTION 10.2.8.12)

17. Special Provisions For Exhibitions Halls
  1. No display or exhibit shall be installed to any required exit or access to fire fighting equipment.
  2. All display of combustible materials must be protected.
  3. Any place used for display shall be equipped with an AFSS when the area exceeds 1394 m².
  4. Combustible material not on display, must be stored with enclosure of at least 2 hours rating and protected by an automatic fire extinguishing system.

(SECTION 10.2.8.15)
18. Projection Rooms For Safety Film
  1. Every projection rooms must be of permanent construction. The rooms shall have a floor area of not less than 7.4m² for single machine and at least 3.75m² for each additional machine.
  2. Each motion picture projector, flood light or similar pieces of equipment shall have a clear working spaces not less than 2.25m².
  3. Projection Room shall be at least 1 outswinging, self closing door not < 76cm wide by 2 m high.
  4. Aggregate of parts and openings for projection equipment must be 25% of area of the wall between the projection room and the auditorium. All openings shall be provided with glass or other approved materials.

    (SECTION 10.2.8.17)

  5. Projection booth room ventilation shall be not less than the following:

    1. Supply Air - 2 or more, located at opposite ends of the room

    2. Exhaust Air- 1 or more

  6. Each projection machine shall be provided with an exhaust duct which will exhaust directly to the outside of the building.

    1. Electric Arc Projection Equipment - capacity = 5.66m³ per minute for each lamp.

    2. Xenon Projection Equipment - capacity = 8.5m³ minute per lamp. The external temperature must be less than 54.5°C.

  7. Maximum of 4 container for flammable liquids not greater than 0.5 liters.
  8. Appurtenant electrical equipment such as transformers may be located within or in a separate room of equivalent of fire resistance construction.
19. Open Flame

No open flames to be used in any place of assembly except when used in conjunction with approved heating or cooking appliances or under written permit from City/Municipal fire marshal. (SECTION 10.2.8.18)

20. Special Provisions for Food Service Establishment

All devices in connection with the preparation of food shall be installed and operated to avoid hazard to the safety of occupants, and shall be installed and maintained in accordance with good engineering practice.
(SECTION 10.2.8.9 para C)
