FIRE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS OF RESIDENTIAL OCCUPANCY
HOTELS AND DORMITORIES OCCUPANCY
REQUIREMENTS FIRE CODE REQUIREMENTS
1. Means of Egress
(Fire Exit)
a. Number
(Means of Egress)
  1. Atleast 2 exit remote from each other
  2. Atleast 3 exit remote from each other for 500 persons but not more than 1000 persons
  3. Atleast 4 exit remote from each other for 1000 persons or more
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para G)
b. Minimum Corridor Width
(Means of Egress)

Sufficient width to accommodate the required occupant load and shall not be less than 112 cm

(SECTION 10.2.14.3 para B.3)
c. Capacity
(Means of Egress)
  1. Stairway - One person for every 7.6mm.
  2. Level components and ramps- One person for every 5mm.
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para C)
d. Occupant Load
(Means of Egress)

1 person for every 18.6 sq. m


(SECTION 10.2.14.2 para A)
e. Size
(Means of Egress)

Not less than 915mm


(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para F)
f. Stairwidth
(Means of Egress)

<50 persons - 915 mm
<2000 persons - 1120mm
≥2000 persons - 1420mm

(SECTION 10.2.5.4 para B.2)
g. Arrangement/ Remoteness
(Means of Egress)

1. Without supervised automatic sprinkler system
 • 1/2 of the maximum overall diagonal dimension

2. With supervised automatic sprinkler system
 • 1/3 of the maximum overall diagonal dimension
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para H)

3. Common path of travel shall not exceed 10m.

4. Dead end corridors shall not exceed 6m.
(SECTION 10.2.14.3 para B.6)

h. Travel Distance
(Means of Egress)
  1. Without supervised automatic sprinkler system  • not exceeding by 23m within guest room to corridor door
  2. With supervised automatic sprinkler system  • not exceeding by 38m within guest room to corridor door
  3. Not more than 30m from door to any room to exit
(SECTION 10.2.14.3 para B.5)
i. Discharge
(Means of Egress)

All required exits shall terminate directly to the street or through yard, court or passageway with protected openings

(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para M)
2. Door
  1. A door designed to be kept normally closed in a means of egress, such as a door to a stair or horizontal exit, shall be provided with a reliable self-closing mechanism. A door designed to be kept normally closed shall bear a sign reading substantially as follows:
    FIRE EXIT
    KEEP DOOR CLOSED
  2. Doors on buildings of 4 or more storeys in height shall be provided with re-entry mechanism to provide access out of the stairway to another exit.
  3. Doors not allowing re-entry shall be provided with a sign indicating the location of the nearest door, in each direction of travel that allows reentry or exit.
    NO RE-ENTRY
    PROCEED TO _____FLOOR
    AND______FLOOR
    (SECTION 10.2.5.3 para C)
  4. Doors between guest rooms and corridors shall be self-closing.
    (SECTION 10.2.14.3 para B.6)
  5. Each guest room shall be provided with a door having a fire protection rating at least twenty (20) minutes.
    (SECTION 10.2.14.3 para C.2)
3. Lightings and Signs
  1. Illumination of means of egress.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.11)
  2. All signs must be illuminated. “EXIT”, “NOT AN EXIT”,”TO BASEMENT”, “STORE ROOM”,”LINEN CLOSET” with directional signs. (SECTION 10.2.5.12)
  3. Exit sign must be not less than 15cm height and 19mm width; In existing buildings not less than 11.5cm height.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.12 para C)

4. Emergency Evacuation Plan

Emergency Evacuation Plan shall be provided in accordance with Section 10.2.5.13 of this RIRR.
(SECTION 10.2.14.3 para B.9)

5. Protection of Guest Rooms
  1. New Hotel building every corridors hall be separated from guest rooms by at least 1 hour fire resistance.
  2. Each guest room shall have a door having a 20 mins fire rating.
  3. Openings in corridor partitions other than door openings shall be prohibited
  4. Doors that open directly onto exit access.
(SECTION 10.2.14.3 para C.2)
6. Fire Detection, Alarm and Communication System
  1. Manual - Hotel and Dormitories with less than 15 guest.
  2. Automatic - Hotel and Dormitories with 15 or more guests.
(SECTION 10.2.14.3 para C.4)

7. Automatic Fire Suppression System (AFSS)
  1. Hotels and dormitories with four (4) storeys and below shall be installed with sprinkler system in accordance with NFPA 13R.
  2. Hotels and dormitories with five (5) storeys and above shall be installed with sprinkler system in accordance with NFPA 13.
    (SECTION 10.2.14.3 para C.5)
  3. Where a hazard is high, 1-hour fireresistive construction and self closing door or automatic fire suppression system shall be used. Hazardous area includes: Boiler and heater rooms; Laundries; Repair shops; Rooms or spaces used storage of combustible supplies and equipment
    (SECTION 10.2.14.3 para C.7)
8. Extinguishing Requirements
  1. Low hazard - 1 unit per 200 square meters, travel distance of 15m
  2. Moderate hazard - 1 unit per 100 square meters, travel distance of 12m
  3. High hazard - 1 unit per 75 square meters 1 unit every 10 meters of travel distance
(SECTION 10.2.6.9 para G)
9. Standpipe

The design, installation and maintenance of standpipe systems shall be in accordance with NFPA 14, Standard for the Installation of Standpipe, Private Hydrant and Hose Systems.

(SECTION 10.2.6.8)
FIRE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS OF APARTMENT BUILDING OCCUPANCY
REQUIREMENTS FIRE CODE REQUIREMENTS
1. Means of Egress
(Fire Exit)
a. Number of Fire Exits
(Means of Egress)

Every living unit shall have access to atleast two (2) separate exits. Exceptions

1. Any living unit, which has an exit directly to the street or yard at ground level or by way of an outside stairway that serve a maximum of two units or an enclosed stairway that serve a maiximum of two units with fire rating of 1 hour.

2. Apartment buildings not more than 3 storeys , not more than 6 living units per floor, with smoke proof enclosure, immediately accessible to all apartmetns served.

3. Any building not more than 3 storeys in height with at least 1 hour fire resistance, under the following conditions:

  1. Stairway is completely enclosed with a partition having at least 1 hour with self closing door
  2. Stairway does not serve any floor below the floor of exit discharge
  3. Corridors serving as access to exits at least 1 hour fire resistance
  4. Not more than 6.10m of travel distance to reach an exit from entrance door of any living unit.

(SECTION 10.2.14.4 para B.2)
b. Minimum Corridor Width
(Means of Egress)
  1. Corridors with a required capacity of more than 50 persons shall not be less than 112cm.
  2. Corridors with a required capacity of not more than 50 persons shall not be less than 91cm.
(SECTION 10.2.14.4 para B3)
