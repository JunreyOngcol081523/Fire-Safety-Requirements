General Requirement
  1. No board and care occupancy shall have its sole means of egress or means of escape pass through any nonresidential or non-health care occupancy in the same building.
  2. No board and care occupancy shall be located above a non-residential or non-health care occupancy, unless the board and care occupancy and exits therefrom are separated from the nonresidential or non-health care occupancy by construction having a minimum (2-hr) fire resistance rating.
(SECTION 10.2.12.1)
1. Scope

This section shall apply to residential board and care occupancies
≤ (16) residents = small facility
> (16) residents = large facility

(SECTION 10.2.12.2 para A)
2. Means of Escape
  1. Shall be free of all obstructions or impediments.
  2. Sleeping room and living area shall have access to a primary means of escape.
  3. Sleeping rooms or living areas are above, the primary means of escape shall be:   - an interior stair,
      - an exterior stair,
      - a horizontal exit, or a fire escape stair.
  4. Sleeping rooms and living areas in facilities without a sprinkler system shall have a second means of escape consisting of any of the following:
    1. Door, stairway, passage, or hall providing a way of unobstructed travel to the outside of the dwelling at street or the finished ground level
    2. Adjacent non-lockable space
    3. Outside window or door operable from the inside, without the use of tools, keys, or special effort,

      ≥(510mm) = clear width and
      ≥(800mm) = height, and
      ≤(820mm) = bottom of the opening above the floor.
    4. Sleeping rooms that have a door leading directly to the outside of the building with access to the finished ground level or to an exterior stairway:
       - shall be considered as meeting all the requirements for a second means of escape.
    5. Sleeping rooms shall not be required to have a secondary means of escape provided all of the following are met:
      1. Protected throughout by an approved, automatic sprinkler system
      2. A fire alarm system is provided.
      3. (1.83m) = width required for exit access for aisles, corridors and ramps
(SECTION 10.2.12.2 para B)

3. Interior Stairs Used for Primary Means of Escape
  1. Shall be enclosed with fire barriers having a minimum of (1⁄2-hr) fire resistance rating.
  2. Shall comply with Section 10.2.5.4 of this RIRR
    < 2,000 persons = 1,120mm width of stair
    ≥ 2,000 persons = 1,420mm width of stair
  3. Primary means of escape shall be arranged so that occupants are not required to pass through a portion of a storey above,
    - unless that route is separated from all spaces on that storey by construction having a minimum of (1⁄2-hr) fire resistance rating.
  4. Stairs that connect a storey at street level to only one other storey shall be permitted to be open to the storey that is not at street level.
  5. In buildings (3) or fewer storeys in height and protected by an approved, automatic sprinkler system,
    - stair enclosures shall not be required,
    - provided that there still remains a primary means of escape
    -unless that route is separated from all spaces on that floor by construction having a minimum of (1⁄2-hr) fire resistance rating.
  6. Stairs serving a maximum of (2) storeys in buildings protected with an approved, automatic sprinkler system shall be permitted to be unenclosed.
(SECTION 10.2.12.2 para C)

4. Doors
  1. ≥(800mm) wide.
  2. ≥(610mm) wide = Bathroom doors.
  3. Doors shall be swinging or sliding.
  4. Every closet door latch shall be readily opened from inside.
  5. Every bathroom door shall be designed to allow opening from the outside during an emergency, when locked.
  6. No door in any means of escape, other than those meeting the requirements below, shall be locked against egress when the building is occupied.
  1. Delayed-egress locks shall be permitted on exterior doors only.
  2. Access-controlled egress door assemblies shall be permitted.
  3. Door-locking arrangements shall be permitted provided all of the following conditions are met:
    1. Staff can readily unlock doors at all times.
    2. Protected by an approved, automatic sprinkler system.

(SECTION 10.2.12.2 para D)
5. Protection of Vertical Opening

(1⁄2-hr) = fire resistance rating smoke partitions.
(SECTION 10.2.12.2 para E.1)

6. Hazardous Areas

(1) hour fire resistance rating and an automatic detection and alarm system or automatic sprinkler system required.

-areas for cartoned storage,

-food or household maintenance items in wholesale or institutional-type quantities and concentrations,

-or mass storage of residents’ belongings,
-or other hazardous areas

(SECTION 10.2.12.2 para E.2)
7. Detection, Alarm and Communication System

Manual fire alarm system = shall be provided for small residential custodian care facilities

- all levels including basements, living areas and sleeping rooms shall be provided single station smoke detectors.


(SECTION 10.2.12.2 para E.3)

8. Extinguishing Requirements

≥(4) = storeys in height building shall be protected with approved, supervised sprinkler system, using quick-response or residential sprinklers in accordance with Section 10.2.6.7 of this RIRR.

Portable fire extinguishers shall be installed in accordance with Section 10.2.6.9 of this RIRR.

(SECTION 10.2.12.2 para E.4)
9. Construction of Corridor Walls

(1⁄2-hr) = fire resistance rating walls separating sleeping rooms.

(1⁄2-hr) = fire resistance rating partitioning is finished on both sides with lath and plaster or materials providing a (15-min) thermal barrier.

(44 mm) = thick sleeping room doors, solid-bonded wood-core construction, or of other construction of equal or greater stability and fire integrity.

Any vision panels shall be fixed fire window assemblies or shall be wired glass ≤ (0.84m²) each in area and installed in approved frames.
