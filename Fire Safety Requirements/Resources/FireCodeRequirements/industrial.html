b. Capacity

b. Capacity
(Means of Egress)		 One (1) person for every 9.3 sq.m
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para D and SECTION 10.2.17.1 para A)
d. Size
(Means of Egress)		 Stair way - 1120mm
(SECTION 10.2.5.4 para B2)
e. Arrangement/Remoteness
(Means of Egress)		 1/2 of the maximum overall diagonal dimension
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para H)
f. Travel Distance
(Means of Egress)		 ≤ 61 meters - automatic fire suppression system
≤ 76 meters - approved supervised sprinkle system
(SECTION 10.2.17.2 para F1)
g. Discharge
(Means of Egress)

Terminate directly at a public way or at an exterior exit discharge
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para M)

One exit may discharge thru the ground floor provided:

  1. Ground floor is free and unobstructed.
  2. Ground floor is protected with AFSS.
  3. Discharge on the ground floor is separated from other areas by two (2) hour fire rated construction.
(SECTION 10.2.17.2 para G)

  1. Signs designating exit or ways of travel

4. Fire Detection, Alarm and Communication System

Automatic detection and alarm system is required on all industrial occupancies with 25 or more employees.

(SECTION 10.2.17.3 para C1)
5. Automatic Fire Suppression System(AFSS)

Every high hazard industrial occupancy shall be protected with AFSS appropriate to the particular hazard in accordance with NFPA or other internationally accepted standards.

(SECTION 10.2.17.3 para D1)
6. Fire Extinguisher

Shall be installed in accordance with Section 10.2.6.9 para G of the RIRR

(SECTION 10.2.17.3 para D)
7. Standpipe

The design, installation and maintenance of standpipe systems shall be in accordance with NFPA 14, Standard for the Installation of Standpipe, Private Hydrant and Hose Systems.

(SECTION 10.2.6.8 para A)
8. Hazardous Storage
  1. Any process, operation or storage having a degree of hazard than the normal to the general occupancy shall be enclosed with at least four (4) hours fire resistance rating.
    (SECTION 10.2.6.10 para A)
  2. All construction enclosure for hazardous storage shall have two (2) hours fire resistance and with self closing or automatic fire door.
    (SECTION 10.2.6.10 para B)
9. Explosion Prevention

Explosion prevention in industrial occupancies shall be in accordance with NFPA 654, Standard for the Prevention of Fires for Dust Explosions from the Manufacturing, processing, and Handling of Combustible Particular Solids, and NFPA 69, Standard on Explosion Prevention Systems.

(SECTION 10.2.17.3 para E)
