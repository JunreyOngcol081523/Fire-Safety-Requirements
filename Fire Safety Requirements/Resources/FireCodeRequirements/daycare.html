1. General
  1. The requirements of this Section shall apply to day care occupancies in which
    >(12) clients receive care, maintenance, and supervision by other than their relative(s) or legal -guardian(s) for < (24) hours per day.
  2. The requirements of Sections 10.2.10.1, 10.2.10.4, 10.2.10.7 and 10.2.10.8 of this RIRR shall apply to day care homes.
  3. Where a facility houses > (1) age group, the strictest requirements applicable to any group present shall apply throughout the day care occupancy or building,
  4. Places of religious worship shall not be required to meet the provisions of this Section where operating a day care home while services are being held in the building.
  5. No day care center shall be located below the level of exit discharge.

13. Vertical Openings
  1. Any vertical opening, other than unprotected vertical openings in accordance with para 2 hereof, shall be enclosed or protected in accordance with Section 10.2.6.5 of this RIRR.
  2. Unenclosed vertical openings not concealed within the building construction shall be permitted as follows:
    1. Such openings shall connect < (2) adjacent storeys (one (1) floor pierced only).
    2. Such openings shall be separated from unprotected vertical openings serving other floors by a barrier.
    3. Such openings shall be separated from corridors.

(SECTION 10.2.10.6 para A)
14. Detection, Alarm and Communication Systems
  1. Day care occupancies shall be provided with an automatic fire detection and alarm system in accordance with Section 10.2.6.6 of this RIRR,
  2. A smoke detection system shall be installed in day care occupancies, other than those housed in one (1) room having at least one (1) door opening directly to the outside at grade plane or to an exterior exit access, and such system shall comply with both of the following:
    1. Detectors shall be installed on each storey in front of the doors to the stairways and in the corridors of all floors occupied by the day care occupancy.
    2. Detectors shall be installed in lounges, recreation areas, and sleeping rooms in the day care occupancy.

(SECTION 10.2.10.6 para B)

15. Extinguishing Requirements
  1. Buildings with unprotected openings, up to (5) communicating floor levels not considered as a high rise building, shall be protected throughout by an approved, supervised automatic sprinkler system in accordance with Section 10.2.6.7 of this RIRR.
  2. Portable fire extinguishers shall also be provided pursuant to Section 10.2.6.9 of this RIRR.

(SECTION 10.2.10.6 para C)
16. Hazardous Areas

Rooms or spaces for the storage, processing, or use of materials specified in para 1 through 3 below shall be protected in accordance with the following:

  1. Separation from the remainder of the building by fire barriers having a minimum (1) hour fire resistance rating or protection of such rooms by automatic extinguishing systems in the following areas:
    1. Boiler and furnace rooms,
    2. Storage of combustible supplies in quantities deemed hazardous by the C/MFM having jurisdiction;
    3. Storage of hazardous materials or flammable or combustible liquids
    4. Janitor closets.
    2. (SECTION 10.2.10.6 para D)

  2. Separation from the remainder of the building by fire barriers having a minimum of (1) hour fire resistance rating and protection of such rooms by automatic extinguishing systems in the following areas:

    1. Laundry rooms.
    2. Maintenance shops, including woodworking and painting areas.
    3. Rooms or spaces used for processing or use of combustible supplies.
    4. Rooms or spaces used for processing or use of hazardous materials or flammable or combustible liquids in quantities deemed hazardous by recognized standards.
  3. Where automatic extinguishing is used to meet the protection requirements of paras 1 and 2, as permitted.

B U I L D I N G S E R V I C E S EQUIPMENT
1. Utilities
  1. Utilities shall comply with the provisions of Section 10.2.7.1 of this RIRR.
  2. Special protective covers for all electrical receptacles shall be installed in all areas occupied by clients.
(SECTION 10.2.10.8 para A)
2. Heating, Ventilating and Air-Conditioning Equipment
  1. Heating, ventilating, and air-conditioning equipment shall be in accordance with Section 10.2.7.2 of this RIRR.

    A. Design and installation shall be in accordance with the latest edition of PMEC.

    B. Shall be provided with automatic fire dampers.

  2. Unvented fuel-fired heating equipment, other than gas space heaters in compliance with NFPA 54/ANSI Z223.1: National Fuel Gas Code, shall be prohibited.
  3. Any heating equipment in spaces occupied by clients shall be provided with partitions, screens, or other means to protect clients from hot surfaces and open flames; if solid partitions are used to provide such protection, provisions shall be made to ensure adequate air for combustion and ventilation for the heating equipment.

(SECTION 10.2.10.8 para B)

3. Elevators, Escalators and Conveyors

Elevators, escalators and conveyors, other than those in day care homes, shall comply with the provisions of Section 10.2.7.5 of this RIRR.

  1. Shall be in accordance with the latest edition of PMEC.
  2. All new elevators shall conform to (ASME) A17.1, Safety Code for Elevators and Escalators, except buildings of less than (5) storeys in height.
  3. Shall be subject to periodic inspections and tests by building management,

All elevators equipped with firefighters’ emergency operation in accordance with para “B” hereof shall be subject to a monthly operation with a written record of the findings made and kept by the building management.

(SECTION 10.2.10.8 para C)
4. Waste Chutes, Incinerators, and Laundry Chutes

Waste chutes, incinerators, and laundry chutes, other than those in day care homes, shall comply with the provisions of Section 10.2.7.4 of this RIRR.

1. General

Rooms used for preschoolers shall not be located above or below the floor of exit discharge.

(SECTION 10.2.9.8 SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR PRE-SCHOOLS)

  1. More than (3), but not more than (12), clients receive care, maintenance,
  2. Where a facility houses more than one age group, the strictest requirements applicable to any group present shall apply throughout the day care home or building, as appropriate to a given area, unless the area housing such a group is maintained as a separate fire area.
  3. Facilities that supervise clients on a temporary basis with a parent or guardian in close proximity shall not be required to meet the provisions of this Section.
  4. Places of religious worship shall not be required to meet the provisions of this Section where operating a day care home while services are being held in the building.

2. Classification of Occupancy
  1. Family Day Care Home shall be a day care home in which ≥ (3), but ≤ (7), clients receive care, maintenance, and supervision by other than their relative(s) or legal guardian(s) for ≤ (24) hours per day, generally within a dwelling unit.
  2. Group Day Care Home shall be a day care home in which ≥ (7), but ≤ (12), clients receive care, maintenance, and supervision by other than their relative(s) or legal guardian(s) for ≤ (24) hours per day, generally within a dwelling unit.

(SECTION 10.2.10.9 para B)
3. Location

No day care home shall be located below the level of exit discharge.
(SECTION 10.2.10.9 para C)

4. Occupant Load
  1. In family day care homes, both of the following shall apply:

    a. The minimum staff-to-client ratio shall be ≥ (1) staff for up to (6) clients, including the caretaker’s own children under age (6).

    b. ≤ (2) clients incapable of self-preservation.

  2. In group day care homes, all of the following shall apply:

    a. The minimum staff-to-client ratio shall be ≥ (2) staffs for up to (12) clients.

    b. ≤ (3) clients incapable of self-preservation.

(SECTION 10.2.10.9 para D)

5. Means of Escape Requirements
  1. Means of escape shall comply with those of Single and Two-Family Dwellings.
  2. Every room used for sleeping, living, recreation, education, or dining purposes shall have the number and type of means of escape.
  3. No room or space that is accessible only by a ladder or folding stairs or through a trap door shall be occupied by clients.
  4. In group day care homes where spaces on the storey above the level of exit discharge are used by clients, that storey shall have > (1) means of escape complying with one of the following:

    a. Door leading directly to the outside with access to finished ground level.
    b. Door leading directly to an outside stair to finished ground level.
    c. Interior stair leading directly to the outside with access to finished ground level, separated from other storeys by (1/2) hour fire barrier.

  5. Where clients occupy a storey below the level of exit discharge, that storey shall have ≥ (1) means of escape complying with any of the following:
    (SECTION 10.2.10.9 para E)

    a. Door leading directly to the outside with access to finished ground level.
    b. Door leading directly to an outside stair going to finished ground level.
    c. Bulkhead enclosure.
    d. Interior stair leading directly to the outside with access to finished ground level, separated from other storeys by (1/2) hour fire barrier.
    (SECTION 10.2.10.9 para E)

6. Arrangement of Means of Escape
  1. Dead-end corridors shall ≤ (6m).
  2. Doors in means of escape shall be protected from obstructions.

(SECTION 10.2.10.9 para F)
6. Travel Distance
  1. Travel distance shall be measured in accordance with Section 10.2.5.2 of this RIRR.
  2. ≤ (46m) = between any point in a room and a door leading directly to the outside with access to finished ground level
  3. ≤ (15m) = between any point in a sleeping room and access to a means of escape from that room
  4. ≥ (15m) = in buildings protected throughout by an approved, supervised automatic sprinkler.

(SECTION 10.2.10.9 para G)

8. Protection
  1. Doorway between the level of exit discharge and any storey below shall be equipped with a fire door assembly having (20-min) fire protection rating.
  2. Where the storey above the level of exit discharge is used for sleeping purposes, there shall be a fire door assembly having (20-min) fire protection rating at the top or bottom of each stairway.
