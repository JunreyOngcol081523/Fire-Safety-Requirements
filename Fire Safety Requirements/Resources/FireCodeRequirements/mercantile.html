FIRE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS OF MERCANTILE OCCUPANCY
REQUIREMENTS FIRE CODE REQUIREMENTS
1. Means of Egress
(Fire Exit)
a. Number
(Means of Egress)
  1. At least 2 exits remote from each other
  2. At least 3 exits remote from each other for 500 persons but not more 1000 persons
  3. At least 4 exits remote from each other for 1000 persons or more
    (SECTION 10.2.5.2 para G)
  4. 1 exit in Class C if travel distance to exit or to a mall does not exceed 23 for unsprinklered, and 30m for sprinklered.
    (SECTION 10.2.15.2 para D)
b. Capacity
(Means of Egress) 		Stairway - 1 person for every 7.6mm Level component and ramps - 1 person for every 5mm
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para C)
c. Occupant Load (Means of Egress)
  1. Street floor and sales floor below the street floor - 1 person for each 2.8m² gross floor area.
  2. Sales space of different grade of streets - 1 person for each 3.7m² of gross floor area of sales space.
  3. Upper floors used for sale - 1 person for each 5.6m² gross floor area.
  4. Covered walls - 1 person for 2.8m² gross floor area.
  5. Floor used only for offices, storage, shipping and not open to general public - 1 person for each 9.3m² gross floor area
(SECTION 10.2.15.1 para C)
d. Size
(Means of Egress)

Not less than 915mm
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para F)

e. Stairwidth
(Means of Egress) 		New Stair Width
< 500 persons - 915mm
< persons - 1120mm>
≥ persons -1420mm
(SECTION 10.2.5.4 para B.2)

f.Arrangement/ Remoteness
(Means of Egress)

Without supervised automatic sprinkler system – 1/2 of the maximum overall diagonal dimension

With supervised automatic sprinkler system – 1/3 of the maximum overall diagonal dimension


(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para H)
g. Travel Distance
(Means of Egress)

Travel distance to exits shall be measured in accordance with Section 10.2.5.2 of this RIRR.

Exception: An increase in the above travel distance to 46 meters shall be permitted in a building completely protected by an approved, supervised sprinkler system
(SECTION 10.2.15.2 para F)

h. Discharge
(Means of Egress)
  1. Terminate directly at a public way or at an exit discharge.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.2 para M)
  2. If sprinklered, not more than 1/2 may discharge through the street floor.
    (SECTION 10.2.15.2 para G)
2. Door
  1. Doors shall not open directly onto a stair without a landing.
  2. The landing shall have a width not less than the width of the door.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.3 para B)
  3. Shall swing with the exit travel.
    (SECTION 10.2.15.2 para H2)
  4. Not less than 71 cm in clear width.
  5. No single door in a doorway shall exceed 122 cm in width.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.3 para E)

3. Lightings and Signs
  1. Illumination of means of egress
    (SECTION 10.2.5.11)
  2. All signs must be illuminated. “EXIT”, “NOT AN EXIT”,”TO BASEMENT”, “STORE ROOM”,”LINEN CLOSET” with directional signs.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.12)
  3. Exit sign must be not less than 15 cm high and 19 mm wide; in existing buildings not less than 11.5 cm high.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.12 para C)
4. Protection of Vertical Openings

Any stairway, elevator shaft, escalator opening or other critical opening shall be enclosed or protected.
(SECTION 10.2.15.3 para A)

5. Detection, Alarm and Communication System

1. Automatic - Class A (2,788 m² or more) and Class B (less than 2,787 m² but over 279m² )
(SECTION 10.2.15.3 para C)

6. Automatic Fire Suppression System (AFSS)

1. Approved, supervised automatic sprinkler system protection shall be installed all mercantile occupancies as follows:

  • Throughout all mercantile occupancies 3 or more storeys in height provided that atleast one (1) of the floor is atleast 232 m²
  • Throughout all mercantile occupancies exceeding 1,115 square meter in gross area
  • Throughout floor below the street floor having an area exceeding 232 square meter when used for the sale, storage or handling of combustible goods and merchandise.
(SECTION 10.2.15.3 para D)
7. Fire Extinguisher
  1. Low hazard - One unit per 200 square meters, travel distance of 15 m
  2. Moderate hazard - One unit per 100 square meters, travel distance of 12 m
  3. High hazard - One unit per 75 square meters
    1 unit every 10 meters of travel distance.
    (SECTION 10.2.6.9 para G)
8. Hazardous Areas
  1. An area used for general storage shall be separated from other building by construction having a fire resistance rating of not less than 1 hour;
  2. All openings shall be protected with self closing fire doors.
  3. Areas with high hazard contents.
  4. Provide with both fire-resistive construction and automatic fire suppression system.
  5. Cooking equipment shall be protected, unless the cooking equipment is one of the following types:
    1. Outdoor equipment;
    2. Portable equipment;
    3. Equipment used only for food warming.
(SECTION 10.2.15.3 para E)
