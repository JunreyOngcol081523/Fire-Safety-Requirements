FIRE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS OF PLACES OF EDUCATIONAL OCCUPANCY
REQUIREMENTS FIRE CODE REQUIREMENTS
1. Means of Egress (Fire Exit)
a. Number
(Means of Egress)
  1. 1. Every room or space with a capacity of 50 or more persons or over 93m² area shall have at least 2 doorways as remote from each other .
  2. Not less than 2 separate exits shall be provided on every storey.
(SECTION 10.2.9.2 para B)
b. Capacity
(Means of Egress)
  1. Stairway - 1 person for every 7.6mm width
  2. Level Components and Ramps - 5mm width per person
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para C)
c. Occupant Load
(Means of Egress)
  1. one person for each 1.9m² of net classroom area
  2. one person for each 4.6m² of net area of shops, laboratories and other similar rooms
  3. one person for each 3.3 m² of net area of dry nurseries/sleeping facilities
(SECTION 10.2.9.1 para B)
d. Size
(Means of Egress)

New Stair Width

<2000 persons - 1120mm
≥2000 persons - 1420mm
(SECTION 10.2.5.4 para B.2)

e. Arrangement/ Remoteness
(Means of Egress)
  1. Without supervised automatic sprinkler sytem
    • 1/2 of the maximum overall diagonal dimension
  2. With supervised automatic sprinkler system
    • 1/3 of the maximum overall diagonal dimension
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para H)
f. Travel Distance
(Means of Egress)
  1. without supervised automatic sprinkler system
    • not exceeding 46m
  2. with supervised automatic sprinkler system
    •not exceeding 61m
(SECTION 10.2.9.2 para C)
g. Discharge
(Means of Egress)

Terminate directly at a public way or at an exterior exit discharge

(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para M)
h. Dead-End Corridors

Dead-end corridors shalll not exceed 6m

(SECTION 10.2.9.2 para I)
2. Door

Doors in means of egress shall swing in the direction of exit travel.
Every closet door latch shall be open from the inside of the closet.

(SECTION 10.2.5.3 para B)

Not less than 71cm in clear width.
No single door in a doorway shall exceed 122cm in width.

(SECTION 10.2.5.3 para E)

Lightings and Signs
  1. Illumination of means of egress
    (SECTION 10.2.5.11)
  2. All signs must be illuminated. “EXIT”, “NOT AN EXIT”,”TO BASEMENT”, “STORE ROOM”,”LINEN CLOSET” with directional signs.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.12)
  3. Exit sign must be not less than 15cm height and 19mm width; in existing buildings not less than 11.5cm height.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.12 para C)
  4. Flexible plan and open plan buildings and buildings designed for night occupancy and portions of buildings having interior and windowless rooms, areas, and corridors, shall have emergency lighting.
    (SECTION 10.2.9.3 para A)
4. Protection of Vertical Openings

Any interior stairways and other vertical openings in educational buildings shall be enclosed and protected.

(SECTION 10.2.9.5 para A)
5. Fire Detection, Alarm and Communication System
  1. Manual - Every educational building
  2. Automatic - If building is protected with AFSS

6. Automatic Fire Suppression System (AFSS)
  1. Every portion of educational buildings below the floor of exit discharge, being utilized as a classroom or laboratory, shall be protected with approved, supervised sprinkler system in accordance with Section 10.2.6.7 of this RIRR:
      a. throughout the storey containing the classroom or laboratory; and
      b. at the level of exit discharge
  2. When the building is classified as high rise, it shall be completely protected by approved, supervised sprinkler system.
(SECTION 10.2.9.5 para E)
7. Fire Extinguisher
  1. Low hazard - 1 unit per 200 square meters, travel distance of 15m
  2. Moderate hazard - 1 unit per 100 square meters, travel distance of 12m
  3. High hazard - 1 unit per 75 square meters. 1 unit every 10m of travel distance
(SECTION 10.2.6.9 para G)
8. Standpipe

The design, installation and maintenance of standpipe systems shall be in accordance with NFPA 14, Standard for the Installation of Standpipe, Private Hydrant and Hose Systems.
(SECTION 10.2.6.8)

9. Cooking Equipment

Cooking equipment shall be protected in accordance with Section 10.2.7.1 of this RIRR

(SECTION 10.2.9.5 para G)
