9 FIRE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS OF HEALTHCARE OCCUPANCY
FIRE SAFETY REQUIREMENTS OF HEALTH CARE ESTABLISHMENTS
REQUIREMENTS FIRE CODE REQUIREMENTS
1. Fundamental Requirements
  1. All health care buildings shall be designed, constructed, maintained and operated as to minimize the possibility of a fire emergency requiring evacuation.
  2. Recognized that in buildings housing various type of psychiatric patients may be necessary to lock doors and bar windows.
(SECTION 10.2.11.1 para A)
2. Means of Egress
(Fire Exit)
a. Number
(Means of Egress)
  1. At least 2 exit remote from each other.
  2. Atleast 3 exits remote from each other for 500 persons but not more than 1000 persons.
  3. Atleast 4 exits remote from each other for 1000 persons or more.
(SECTION 10.2.11.2 para A.2)
b. Capacity
(Means of Egress)
  1. Health Care (sprinkled) - 7.6mm stairway; 5mm level Components and Ramps (width per person)
  2. Health Care (non sprinkled) - 15mm stairway; 13mm level components and Ramps (width per person)
(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para C, Table 1)
c. Occupant Load
(Means of Egress)
  1. Not less than one (1) persons for each 11.1m2 gross floor area in health care sleeping departments.
  2. Not less than one (1) persons for 22.3m2 of gross floor area of inpatient health care treatment departments.
(SECTION 10.2.11.1 para D)
d. Size
(Means of Egress)

Aisles, corridors and ramps atleast 244cm in Hospitals or Nursing Homes and 183cm for Residential Custodial Care and Adjunct Areas not for treatment.

(SECTION 10.2.11.2 para C.4)

g. Travel Distance
(Means of Egress)
  1. Shall not exceed 30 meters between any room door intended as exit access and an exit.
  2. Shall not exceed 46 meters between any point in a room and an exit.
  3. Shall not exceed 15 meters between any point in a health care sleeping room or suite and an exit access door of that room or suite. The travel distance in A and B may increase by 15m in buildings completely equipped with an AFSS.
(SECTION 10.2.11.2 para C.2)
h. Discharge
(Means of Egress)

Terminate directly at a public way or at an exterior exit discharge, and be in accordance with NFPA 101, Life Safety Code with respect to changes in elevation, stairs, ramps, or keeping means of egress free from obstruction. Means of egress shall be permitted to terminate in an exterior area for detention and correctional occupancies as otherwise provided in NFPA 101.

(SECTION 10.2.5.2 para M)

3. Door
  1. Any door in a fire separation, horizontal exit or a smoke partition may be held open only by an electrical device.
    (SECTION 10.2.11.2 para D)
  2. Exit access doors shall be at least 112cm in hospitals and nursing home, sleeping rooms, diagnostic and treatment rooms or areas such as X-ray, surgery and physical therapy
    (SECTION 10.2.11.2 para D)
4. Location of ICU,OR,DR, ER, etc.

Emergency rooms, operating rooms, intensive care units, delivery rooms and other similar facilities shall be located on the level of exit discharge. When the above requirement is not met, one of the following conditions shall be complied:

1. Where the said facilities are located above the floor of exit discharge but in no case shall exceed five (5) floors, or where the said facilities are located below the floor of exit discharge but in no case shall exceed one (1) floor, it shall be equipped with ramp complying with Section 10.2.5.7 of this RIRR leading directly to exit discharge.

The ramp, constructed in a fire resistive and smoke proof enclosure, shall be so designed to accommodate the movement of hospital beds with non-ambulatory patients to the level of exit discharge. The enclosure shall have the minimum fire rating of two (2) hours.

2. Where the construction of ramp is not practicable, a horizontal exit compliant with Section 10.2.5.6 of this RIRR shall be provided leading to another separate building.
(SECTION 10.2.11.1 para B)

5. Horizontal Exits
  1. At least 2.8m² per occupant in a hospital or nursing home and 1.4m² per occupant in residential-custodial care.
  2. Single door may be used as a horizontal exit if it serves 1 direction and 112cm wide for hospital.
  3. A corridor 2.4m or more in width serving as means of egress shall have the opening protected by a pair of swinging doors, each door must be 1055mm in width.
  4. Approved vision panel is required in each horizontal exit door. Center mullions are prohibited.
(SECTION 10.2.11.2 para F)
6. Lightings and Signs
  1. Illumination of means of egress
    (SECTION 10.2.5.11)
  2. All signs must be illuminated. “EXIT”, “NOT AN EXIT”,”TO BASEMENT”, “STORE ROOM”,”LINEN CLOSET” with directional signs.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.12)
  3. Exit sign must be not less than 15cm high and 19mm wide; in existing buildings not less than 11.5cm high.
    (SECTION 10.2.5.12 para C)
  4. Each nursing home shall have emergency lightning with at least 1 1/2 hour duration.
    (SECTION 10.2.11.2 para H)
7. Emergency Evacuation Plan Emergency Evacuation Plan shall be provided in accordance with Section 10.2.5.13 of this RIRR.
(SECTION 10.2.11.2 para I)
8. Protection of Vertical Openings
  1. Any stairway, ramp, elevator shaft, light and ventilation shaft, chute and other openings between storeys shall be enclosed with fireresistive materials.
  2. A door in a stairway enclosure shall be self-closing, shall normally be kept in closed position and shall be marked in accordance with Section 10.2.5.3 of this IRR.
  3. Fire-stopping shall be provided.
(SECTION 10.2.11.3 para D)

9. Fire Detection, Alarm and Communication System

1. Every building shall have an electrically supervised automatic fire alarm system capable of being manually operated.
(SECTION 10.2.11.3 para F.1)

10. Automatic Fire Suppression System (AFSS)
  1. Approved, supervised sprinkler system shall be provided throughout all hospitals and nursery home, except 1-storey building with a bed capacity of not exceeding 5
  2. Quick-response sprinklers shall be required in smoke compartments containing patient sleeping rooms.
  3. In light hazard occupancies, required automatic fire suppression systems shall be electrically interconnected with the fire alarm system. The main automatic fire suppression control valve shall be electrically monitored so that at least a local alarm will sound when the valve is closed.
(SECTION 10.2.11.3 para F.3 & 5)
11. Fire Extinguisher

Shall be provided in all healthcare occupancies

  1. Low hazard - 1 unit per 200 square meters, travel distance of 15m
  2. Moderate hazard - 1 unit per 100 square meters, travel distance of 12m
  3. High hazard - 1 unit per 75 square meters 1 unit every 10 meters of travel distance
(SECTION 10.2.6.9 para G)
