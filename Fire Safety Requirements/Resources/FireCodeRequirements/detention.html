f. Travel Distance
(Means of Egress)
  1. Maximum of 30 meters between any room door intended as exit access and an exit.
  2. Maximum 46 meters between any point in a room and an exit.
  3. Maximum travel distance of 10m at any point in a sleeping room to the door in that room.
(SECTION 10.2.13.2 para F)
g. Discharge
(Means of Egress)
  • Exits shall be permitted to discharge into a fenced or walled courtyard, provided that not more than two walls of the courtyard are the building walls from which egress is being made.
  • All exits shall be permitted to discharge through the level of exit discharge.
(SECTION 10.2.13.2 para G)
2. Door
  • Doors shall provide resistance to the passage of smoke. Swinging doors shall be self latching, or the opening resistance of the shall not be less than 22N.
  • Doors in smoke barriers shall conform to the requirements for doors in means of egress and shall have locking and release arrangements according to the applicable use condition.
(SECTION 10.2.13.3 para F6, F7)

    (SECTION 10.2.13.2 para H)
4. Protection of Vertical Openings

Any vertical opening shall be enclosed or protected.

(SECTION 10.2.13.3 para A)
5. Fire Detection, Alarm and Communication System
  1. Every building shall have an electrically supervised automatic fire alarm system capable of being manually operated in accordance with Section 10.2.6.6 of this RIRR.
  2. An approved automatic smoke detection system shall be installed in all resident sleeping areas and adjacent day rooms, activity rooms or contiguous common spaces
  3. ≤4 occupants = smoke detectors are not required
  4. Detectors shall be located in exhaust ducts from cells, behind grilles or in other location
  5. Smoke detector shall not be required in “Use Condition II” open dormitories where staff is present whenever it is occupied
(SECTION 10.2.13.3 para C)

6. Extinguishing Requirements

All buildings classified as Use Condition II, Use Condition III, Use Condition IV, or Use Condition V shall be protected throughout by an approved, supervised automatic sprinkler system in accordance with Section 10.2.6.7 of this RIRR. It shall be supervised and electrically connected to the fire alarm system.

(SECTION 10.2.13.3 para D.1&2)

7. Standpipe

Standpipe and hose systems shall be provided in accordance with Section 10.2.6.8 of this RIRR.

(SECTION 10.2.13.3 para D.4)
8. Fire Extinguisher
  1. Low hazard - 1 unit per 200 square meters, travel distance of 15m
  2. Moderate hazard - 1 unit per 100 square meters, travel distance of 12m
  3. High hazard - 1 unit per 75 square meters 1 unit every 10 meters of travel distance
(SECTION 10.2.6.9 para G)
