🔥 Fire Safety Awareness

Learn how to stay safe and prepared during fire emergencies

🏠 At Home 🏫 At School 🏢 At Work 🏬 Public Places

🏠 Fire Safety at Home

  • Make sure there is an exit and that it is always open and accessible.
  • Prepare your fire extinguisher and know how to operate it properly.
  • Unplug any appliance that is not in use.
  • Avoid overnight charging of electronic devices.
  • Do not overload electrical outlets.
  • Keep the stove clean and avoid placing any items on top of it.
  • Keep matches, lighters, and any chemicals that can cause fire out of reach.
  • Check for LPG leaks regularly; replace the hose if it shows signs of wear or damage.
  • Teach all household members how to perform the "Stop, Drop, and Roll" technique.
  • Know your local emergency numbers and store them on your cellphone.

🏫 Fire Safety at School

  • Always know the school's fire exit routes and assembly areas.
  • Participate seriously during fire drills.
  • Do not block hallways, stairs, or exits with bags or furniture.
  • Report any damaged electrical outlets or exposed wires to the school staff.
  • Remain calm and follow teachers' instructions during a fire emergency.

🏢 Fire Safety at Work

  • Know the location of fire exits, alarms, and fire extinguishers in your workplace.
  • Do not overload electrical sockets or use damaged power cords.
  • Keep flammable materials stored safely and away from heat sources.
  • Join company fire drills and know your designated evacuation area.
  • If you see smoke or fire, sound the alarm and alert your supervisor immediately.

🏬 Fire Safety at Malls, Hotels, or Leisure Places

  • Upon entering, look for the nearest exits and fire extinguishers.
  • Stay calm and follow the building's emergency procedures when alarms sound.
  • Do not use elevators during a fire; use the stairs instead.
  • Assist children, elderly, and persons with disabilities during evacuation.
  • Follow the directions of security or fire safety personnel at all times.
